Fence light planner

Lay out each string's path across the fence in straight runs and right-angle turns. Bulbs are placed at their real spacing along the path, and each string's length is tracked against its limit.

With a string picked, click to lay its path. Lines lock to horizontal or vertical. Drag a point or a whole row to move it; rows move up and down, columns move left and right. Right-click or Esc deselects the string so you can grab any point on any string, or drag empty space to pan. Scroll or pinch to zoom; hold Space or use the middle button to pan. Hold Ctrl to place without snapping. Alt-click a point to cut the string from there on. Tab and Shift+Tab step to the next or previous string.
